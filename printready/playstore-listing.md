## Full description

**PrintReady AI turns dark lecture slides and PDFs into clean, ink-saving printable notes — right on your device.**

Dark-themed lecture slides print badly: heavy backgrounds waste ink and make text hard to read. PrintReady AI analyzes every page and rebuilds it for print:

- **Clear background** — removes dark slide backgrounds and boosts text contrast
- **Black & white / grayscale / invert** — pick the look you want
- **Logo removal** — drag boxes over logos, watermarks or page clutter to erase them
- **A4 N-up sheets** — place 2, 4, 6 or 9 pages on one sheet with optional page numbers and separation lines
- **Merge PDF** — combine several PDFs into one file

Everything runs **on device** — private, offline, no account, no uploads.

### Features

- Convert dark PDFs to clean print-ready notes
- 3 quality levels (~100 / 200 / 300 DPI)
- Per-page selection — convert only the pages you need
- A4 sheet layout with page numbers and separation lines
- Merge multiple PDFs into one
- Share or open the result anywhere
- 100% offline and private

**PrintReady AI — read less, print less, understand more.**
